# EHB 2026 format requirements

Consulted: https://www.ehbconference.ro/Submission.aspx (page heading still says “EHB 2025” in one banner; body text specifies **EHB 2026**).

## Confirmed from the official page (2026-08-19 fetch)

- Language: English (USA).
- Springer IFMBE proceedings; **minimum 6 pages** for individual indexing; **maximum 15 pages**; **80 EUR per page after 6**.
- Note on the page: 6 Springer one-column pages ≈ 4 IEEE two-column pages.
- Review: **PDF only**, **≤ 2 MB**, EasyChair `https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=ehb2026`.
- Window stated on the page: **1 May 2026 – 30 July 2026**.
- Blind review: submit **without author names and affiliation**.
- Do **not** insert headers, footers, or page numbers; do **not** apply PDF security.
- Figures (camera-ready / Springer): **≥300 dpi images**, **≥600 dpi line drawings**.
- After acceptance, Word format also to papers@ehbconference.ro; official **DOC templates** are linked on the page (blind and with authors).
