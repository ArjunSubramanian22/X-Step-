\title{X-Step: A Low-Cost Four-Site Smart Insole and Machine-Learning Framework for Continuous Plantar-Pressure Risk Monitoring in Diabetic Foot Care}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
We present a four-site FSR insole and leakage-safe ML framework for continuous plantar-pressure \emph{risk monitoring} in diabetic foot care. Current quantitative tables are engineering/simulation validation on a synthetic cohort, not prospective clinical outcomes.
\end{abstract}
\section{Introduction}
DFU mechanics and the limits of episodic screening motivate low-cost continuous monitoring~\cite{armstrong2017,bus2016}.
\section{Related Work}
Smart insoles, DFU guidance, gait wearables, and DFU photography~\cite{iwgdf2023,hegde2016,goyal2018}.
\section{System Architecture}
Four FSR sites (MET1, MET2, MET5, HEEL), ESP32, 28-byte BLE payload, host ML, mobile record.
\section{Methods}
Features, grouped splits, baselines, SAP: see the repository files \texttt{research/METHODS\_FEATURES.md} and \texttt{research/STATISTICAL\_ANALYSIS\_PLAN.md}.
\section{Experiments}
Baselines, ablation, robustness, latency; synthetic data labeled as such.
