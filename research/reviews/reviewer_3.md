# Reviewer 3 — Clinical

**Score: 4 / 10** as a clinical DFU paper; **7 / 10** if repositioned as engineering methods

## Strengths
- Title/abstract now target pressure-risk **monitoring**, not diagnosis.
- IWGDF cited as clinical risk stratification, not as validating the classifier.
- Thresholds named **engineering risk-alert operating points**.
- Ulcer CNN removed from the core paper (unpaired public images).
- LLM cannot set risk.

## Major concerns
- Zero patients, zero ulcers, zero neuropathy scores.
- Cannot support “predicts ulcers,” “prevents amputation,” “clinically validated.”
- 75 kPa / 200 kPa constants must not be sold as IWGDF cut-offs.

## Minor concerns
- Diabetic-foot framing in the introduction may still over-promise if Results stay synthetic-only.
- Temperature RCTs are related work, not X-Step outcomes.

## Likely rejection reasons
Clinical reviewers reject device papers that imply DFU benefit without a protocol, N, and endpoints.

## Required fixes
State in Abstract and Limitations: no human subjects; no outcome trial. Any future study needs ethics review (the walking protocol is **not** IRB approval).

## Last-mile response
Claim language was tightened; citation years/authors corrected; image model scoped out. **Do not submit as a clinical efficacy paper.**
